The original stacked primary logo is available as `public/brand/romina-logo.svg`, extracted from the 22 outlined paths on PDF page 3. It contains no embedded raster image or font dependency. Its symbol/wordmark relationship is preserved; PDF CMYK conversion rounding is normalized to the documented digital red #D42028. Reproduce it with `node scripts/extract-brand-logo.mjs "path/to/brand-guideline.pdf"`. The earlier PNG remains compatible with saved restaurant settings and is mapped to the SVG for public menu and QR rendering. Allow clear space at least the height of the internal icon around the complete logo. The supplied PDF explains this measurement on page 5. Minimum digital widths: primary 70px, secondary 115px. Minimum print widths: primary 25mm, secondary 40mm. Use white or sufficiently contrasting backgrounds. Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, add strokes/shadows or place the mark on busy images. Keep the logo separate from headings. Use graphic patterns sparingly and clean layouts (page 14). Public header and QR print layouts reserve whitespace and use the primary logo above its minimum width.

Poppins is hosted locally from the fontsource package, so builds do not require Google Fonts. Marketing navigation, buttons and body copy use Poppins; the user-selected Cormorant Garamond remains on editorial headings. Navigation labels are 15px, buttons 14px, and supporting copy is enlarged for mobile and desktop. Nokia Pure files and a license were not supplied, so it has not been bundled or misrepresented as installed. Ethiopian text currently uses system fallback fonts. An approved font asset/license is needed for exact Ethiopian typography.

The marketing header pairs the original red emblem with `public/brand/romina-wordmark.svg`: the 17 untouched black letter paths from the primary logo, cropped without a font substitution. Reproduce this asset with `node scripts/extract-brand-wordmark.mjs`. This horizontal header arrangement is an interface adaptation; the supplied stacked primary artwork remains unchanged. On the user-requested transparent navigation, the letter artwork is rendered white for contrast; it returns to black on the white glass navigation after scrolling. The red emblem keeps its original colours and shape.

At the user's request, the guest experience uses locally hosted Cormorant Garamond for display headings and Manrope for body text and controls. The workspace retains Poppins. The original vector logo remains unchanged and transparent; the welcome screen removes its white panel and uses a thin, zero-blur white edge shadow following the artwork for legibility. A red More disclosure at the lower right widens left into a single red navigation strip with white controls, with keyboard dismissal and reduced-motion support.

The guest welcome and menu header now use `public/brand/romina-emblem.svg`, the five original red/white symbol paths extracted from the same primary artwork. The stacked wordmark is omitted there at the user's request, while the full primary logo remains on the marketing site and print cards. The emblem enters with a one-time falling bounce on each welcome entry, then visually travels into the menu header on View Menu. Its shape is preserved throughout, with the restaurant name displayed to its right in the header.

Select the Romina Restaurants brand profile during onboarding to apply the documented red and supplied logo. Other restaurant tenants start neutral and can configure their own logo and primary color in settings. The administration interface has a separate neutral green/slate design system. Restaurant branding does not change authorization or platform administration.

No invented menu records have been added to the development database. Staff can now upload dish/logo images and PDF/image menu documents through authenticated Cloudinary endpoints. The home page uses place-setting and food photography extracted from supplied PDF pages 25 and 24 respectively. Historical example prices in the brand book are not treated as current menu prices. The physical printed QR code must be tested with real devices at its final print size before operational use.

The Romina marketing home page introduces the complete, unmodified primary logo on white with a fine plate ring, then opens two curtains onto the food photograph. The hero copy and navigation follow, completing within 2.35 seconds, once per browser tab session. The intro mounts outside the restaurant-data Suspense boundary so loading restaurant content does not restart it. It does not intercept clicks or focus; interaction dismisses it immediately. It is hidden without JavaScript and in print, and skipped for reduced-motion users or restored scroll positions. Guest menu, other restaurant tenants and staff workspace pages do not receive this marketing intro.

At the user's request, the culinary map journey below “Your next craving” adds a gold accent (#AD803A) for the country or region in focus. Romina red remains on the map borders, controls and prices. The locally served Natural Earth country paths move from the world view to Ethiopia, Europe and Asia with normal scrolling. Published, available dishes are resolved from the existing cuisine categories and their children; photos, names, prices and links use current menu records. No menu data is fabricated. Recognized category names include Ethiopian/Traditional/Habesha, European/Italian/French/Continental/Mediterranean, and Asian/Chinese/Indian/Thai/Japanese/Korean/Vietnamese. Empty destinations show a discreet team enquiry message and the menu link. The global motion control freezes the camera; destination links still work without animation. Reduced-motion, data-saving, no-JavaScript and print views show the cuisines in normal page flow. Map source and rebuild instructions are in `public/maps/README.md`.
